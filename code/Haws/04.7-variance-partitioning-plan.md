# 04.7 variance partitioning: genotype vs. ploidy and pH

**Question:** 04.4 found that genotype similarity between oysters tracks methylation similarity (rho = 0.86), while ploidy and pH effects can't be told apart from label permutations, and the power simulations (04.4, 04.6) put limits on how large those effects could be. This analysis puts the genetics result on a formal footing: **how much of the methylation variation between oysters is explained by genetic background, and how much by treatment?** It's option A in [04-methylation-summary.md](04-methylation-summary.md).

## Inputs (from 04.4 and 04.6, nothing new to prep)

| Data | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CpG methylation | 04.4 `prep-cov5-hiperc99.9-cell5-meth10-noSNP-All` | ≥ 5x, below the nuclear 99.9th-percentile coverage, mean methylation 10–90%, **no BS-SNPer SNP at the C or G in any oyster**. Only CpGs with data in every oyster of the set are used; mito excluded |
| Gene-body methylation | 04.6 `count-hiperc99.9-gene-cb3` | Reads summed per gene body (≥ 3 CpGs), ≥ 10 reads in every oyster |
| Genotype | 04.4 `prep-bssnper/CpG-SNP-genotypes.rds` | Alt allele frequency at **bisulfite-safe** SNP CpGs (no C>T at the C, no G>A at the G), ≥ 10x in every oyster. PCA recomputed within each sample set |

**Avoiding circularity:**
- A SNP at a CpG changes its apparent methylation directly, so the CpG features exclude every CpG with a SNP in any oyster.
- The genotype comes only from SNPs that bisulfite conversion can't create.
- Gene bodies still contain some SNP CpGs, but each is diluted among the gene's other CpGs.

## Analyses

For each sample set (all 24; `DropGeno4`, without the four genetic outliers) and feature set (CpGs, genes):

1. **Whole-methylome tests** on Euclidean distance between oysters' methylation profiles:
   - **PERMANOVA** (`vegan::adonis2`, marginal terms, 9,999 permutations): `~ ploidy * pH + genoPC1..K`. Each term is tested given all the others.
   - **Variance partitioning** (`vegan::varpart`, distance-based RDA, adjusted R²): fractions explained by genotype only [a], shared [b], treatment (ploidy * pH) only [c], and residual [d].
   - **Mantel and partial Mantel** (Spearman, 9,999 permutations): genotype distance vs. methylation distance, alone and controlling for treatment differences. This is the formal version of the rho = 0.86 result.
   - **K** (genotype PCs) = 1, 3 (primary), and 5. With 24 oysters, more PCs overfit; adjusted R² corrects for that.
2. **Per-feature models** of methylation proportion: R² of treatment (ploidy * pH, 3 df), genotype (K = 3 PCs), and both, giving the unique and shared fractions for each CpG or gene, plus F-tests of the unique parts.
   - **Two nulls**, 100 each: treatment labels shuffled (the 04.4 scheme), and genotype PCs shuffled between oysters.
   - With 3 df and 24 oysters, chance alone gives R² ≈ 0.13, so the observed fractions are only meaningful against these nulls.

## What would each outcome mean?

- **Genotype fraction well above its null, treatment fraction at its null:** methylation differences between these oysters are mostly genetic, and ploidy and pH add nothing detectable. That's what 04.4 suggests.
- **The genotype fraction drops to its null in `DropGeno4`:** the genetics result comes from the four outlier oysters (possibly a different family or source), not from background variation across the population. The 04.4 rho of 0.665 among the 20 suggests it won't drop to zero.
- **Treatment above its null, once genotype is controlled:** genotype was masking a treatment effect. The 04.4 genotype-covariate run argues against that for pH.

## Limitations

- **Genotype PCs summarize genome-wide relatedness, not local (cis) genotype.** If methylation at a gene is controlled by nearby variants, a per-gene local-genotype model would explain more. That's the follow-up (option C), and `per-gene-partition-*.csv` is its starting point.
- **24 oysters.** These estimates have wide uncertainty, and the K = 1/3/5 comparison shows how sensitive they are to the number of PCs.
- **Distances use methylation proportions,** so oysters with lower coverage add more binomial noise. The 04.4 QC found that depth doesn't explain the PCA structure.
